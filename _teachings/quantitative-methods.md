## Course Overview

This course, for M.Sc. students, covers descriptive and inferential statistics for data analysis, combining theory with hands-on practice. Students learn to:

- Summarize and describe data
- Formulate and conduct hypothesis tests
- Apply a range of statistical analyses using **R** and **Python**

I have served as a teaching assistant for this course across multiple semesters since October 2024.
