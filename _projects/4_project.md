Do large language models hold their answers under pressure? In **Who Flips?** we probe the robustness of LLMs by challenging their answers with counterarguments generated both by the model itself and by other models. We find that such counterarguments can flip model answers, exposing an underlying **answer instability** that has implications for the reliability and safety of LLM-based systems {% cite nikeghbal2026whoflips %}.
