I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/en/) (TUM), where I am advised by [Prof. Dr. Jana Diesner](https://www.professoren.tum.de/en/diesner-jana) at the [Chair of Human-Centered Computing](https://www.cs.cit.tum.de/en/hcc/home/). My research sits at the intersection of **AI safety**, **societal bias in AI**, **natural language processing**, and **computational social science**.

I build methods to understand and mitigate the harms of large language models — probing when and how models reveal hidden societal biases, evaluating their behavior across languages and modalities, and studying their reliability under adversarial and interactive conditions. My recent work includes [CoBia](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.84/) (EMNLP 2025), which uses constructed conversations to surface concealed biases in LLMs, and [MEXA](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1385/) (ACL Findings 2025), a method for evaluating the multilingual capabilities of English-centric LLMs.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.edu/) and my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from [Ferdowsi University of Mashhad](https://en.um.ac.ir/).
